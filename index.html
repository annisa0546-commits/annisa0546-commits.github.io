01 / About

Quietly curious.
Intentionally useful.

I believe a library is more than a place to find information. It is a place to ask better questions, build confidence, and turn information into understanding.

“A good reference conversation can be the beginning of a much bigger discovery.”

I’m Annisa, a librarian in the Library & Knowledge Center (LKC) at BINUS University, Indonesia. My work focuses on reference services and helping students, lecturers, and researchers connect with information that supports learning and scholarship.

I’m interested in how academic libraries can make research feel more approachable: through thoughtful guidance, information literacy, digital collections, and services that respond to the way people actually search and learn.

This website is my evolving professional notebook—a place to bring together the ideas, projects, and questions shaping my practice as a librarian.
